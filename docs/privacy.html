LocalShare privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. LocalShare is made by DEMON GOD (@demongod), a solo developer in India.

The short version

LocalShare does not collect, send or sell any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server run by me. Everything stays on your phone and the devices you let in.

What the app does with your data

  • Files and folders. Only the folders you pick are shared, and only over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot, to devices that join. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.
  • Screen and sound. If you start screen sharing, your screen (and, if you allow the microphone permission, the sound your phone plays) is sent live to the devices you let in. It is never recorded or stored. Android asks for permission every time.
  • Microphone permission. Android requires it to capture the sound your phone plays while you share your screen. The microphone itself is used only if you switch on "Also send my microphone" before starting a screen share; then your voice is sent live, with the screen, to the devices you let in. It is never recorded or stored, and it stops when you stop sharing.
  • Chat and TV remote. Live chat messages and pictures stay in the phone's memory while sharing is on and are gone when you stop. A guest can press the TV remote only on devices you allowed.
  • Names and devices. A guest's chosen name, device type and IP address are kept in memory while connected, and in the activity log on your phone. Game scores (the hall of fame), rules, invite links and the optional Wi-Fi name/password you type for the QR code are stored on your phone only.
  • Notifications show that sharing is on. Battery setting keeps sharing alive with the screen off.

Third parties

The QR scanner uses Google Play services' code scanner on your device. LocalShare itself has no tracking or ad code. Donations, if you choose to make one, are handled by the payment app you pick.

Your control

Stop sharing at any time from the app, the notification, or the Quick Settings tile. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

LocalShare is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
